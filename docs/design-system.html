Five rules for a phone-first site

Calling and booking are always in thumb reach
A docked bar at the bottom of every page holds Call, WhatsApp, Packages and Menu. Nothing important lives only in the top corner.
Every tap target is at least 48px
Buttons are 52px tall, chips and links 44px, with at least 8px between neighbours. No hover-only actions.
One main action per screen
A single filled maroon button leads the eye. Everything else is outlined or a text link.
Body text is 16px or larger
Lines stay short, contrast stays at 4.5:1 or better, and the layout survives large system text sizes.
Light on data, heavy on clarity
Prices, units and ranges are set in a monospaced face like a lab report. Long test lists fold away until someone opens them.
