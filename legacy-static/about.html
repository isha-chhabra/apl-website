Our Story

Over two decades of bringing accurate, accessible pathology to Ankleshwar.

Since 2005

One of Ankleshwar's Oldest Pathology Labs

Aakash Pathology Laboratory opened in 2005, at a time when Ankleshwar had few dedicated diagnostic labs of its own. Patients needing specialised tests often had to travel to Surat or Vadodara just to get answers about their health.

Two decades later, that's no longer true for most of this city. What started as a single lab has grown into a network of one main laboratory and three collection centres, all built on the same three principles the founder started with: honest reporting, technical discipline, and treating every sample as if it belonged to your own family.

A Few Firsts

What We Brought to Ankleshwar First

Specialised hormone testing, thyroid, Vitamin B12, Vitamin D3, and fertility hormones, years before most local labs offered it.

Advanced cardiac markers, helping local doctors catch heart risk earlier without sending patients out of town.

Sonography-guided FNAC, an outpatient procedure for diagnosing lumps and swellings without surgery.

Nephelometry testing for kidney function and rheumatoid arthritis, a more precise method than was locally available.

What We Offer

Specialised Diagnostics Under One Roof

From everyday screenings to specialised investigations. Tap an icon to see what it covers.

Note: paternity testing and sex determination are not performed at this laboratory. Some tests may be outsourced to nearby reputed labs.

  • Hormone Tests. Thyroid (T3, T4, TSH), Vitamin B12, D3, fertility tests (AMH), testosterone.
  • Cardiac Tests. Cholesterol, lipid profile, Troponin I, HS-CRP, Homocysteine.
  • Diabetes Testing. HbA1c, fasting and post-meal blood sugar testing.
  • Infectious Disease Testing. Malaria, typhoid, hepatitis B, COVID-19, dengue, HIV.
  • FNAC & Biopsy. Including sonography-guided FNAC for outpatient diagnosis.
  • Culture & Sensitivity. Detecting bacteria and recommending antibiotics in urine, blood, and body fluids.
  • Nephelometry. Accurate diagnosis of renal conditions and rheumatoid arthritis.
  • Bone Marrow Reporting. For diagnosing complicated anemia and other blood conditions.
  • Genetic Tests. Double, triple & quadruple marker, chromosomal analysis, karyotyping.
  • Cancer Markers & Allergy. PSA, CA-125, CEA, plus in-vitro allergy testing.

What Drives Us

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the lab Ankleshwar turns to first, not because we're the only option, but because two decades of consistent, accurate results have made us the obvious one. We want every family in this district to have a trusted name in diagnostics close to home, instead of a long drive to a bigger city.

Our Mission

Every day, we run each test with the same care whether it's a routine check-up or a complex diagnosis, put a qualified pathologist's eyes on every report before it leaves the lab, and keep our prices honest so quality testing never depends on what someone can afford. That's the work behind the vision, done one patient at a time.
