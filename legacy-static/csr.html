Corporate Social Responsibility

We understand the need to conduct business ethically and work towards the betterment of society. In keeping with that, we've always offered heavy discounts to those in need, those serving in uniform, and the relatives of police and defence personnel.

Support for the LGBTQIA+ Community

We work with Sakhi Char Chowghi Trust, an NGO based in Malad, Mumbai, dedicated to the welfare of the Queer (LGBTQIA+) community. We've donated fans and a refrigerator for medicine preservation, supporting people affected by the community's needs, including those living with HIV/AIDS.

Treatment Support for a Young Patient

We collaborated and contributed to the treatment of a girl from a tribal community with serious surgical needs, at S.I.D.S. Hospital, Surat.

Food Security for Women and Children

We contribute to Bhukhiya nu Bhojan, a local charitable organisation providing free food to those in need, with special emphasis on women and children.

Road Safety with the Traffic Police

We believe timely, appropriate measures prevent unfortunate incidents. In partnership with the traffic police department, we've applied free reflectors, stickers, and fluorescent materials to cyclists, tractors, and dumpster vehicles that often go without them, reducing accidents caused by poor visibility at night.

Holding the Line During COVID-19

We did not raise testing prices during the pandemic, despite a worldwide increase in the cost of diagnostic chemicals and input materials. We also fully complied with the government's price cap on RT-PCR testing throughout that period.

Want to Know More About Our Community Work?

We're happy to talk about it, or to hear suggestions for what we should do next.
